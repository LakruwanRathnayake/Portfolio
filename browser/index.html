I'm a passionate full-stack Software Engineer with 3+ years of experience building production-grade applications. Currently at KIK Lanka (PVT) LTD, I architect scalable systems, build real-time collaborative tools, and deploy cloud-native solutions on AWS and DigitalOcean.

Outside of code, I'm driven by clean architecture, elegant UI, and continuous learning. If there's a complex problem to solve, I'm already excited to break it down.
